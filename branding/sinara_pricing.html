Pricing Rationale

Why ¥4,980 for Pro?

Hotel operations managers have a monthly software budget. ¥4,980 (~$33) is below the "needs approval" threshold in most small hotels. Low enough to self-purchase, high enough to signal real value. Comparable to Deputy or When I Work internationally.

Why OCR behind a paywall?

Qwen VL-OCR has a per-call cost. Keeping it Pro+ protects your margins. Also, OCR is a strong upgrade motivator — once a manager sees how fast scanning works vs. manual entry, the paywall converts.

Why 3 divisions on Pro?

Most mid-size hotels have F&B, Front Desk, and Housekeeping — exactly 3. This makes Pro feel like it was designed for them without going unlimited, which keeps Business tier appealing for full properties.

Future: per-property pricing

When targeting hotel chains or groups, consider switching to a per-property model (¥4,980 × N properties). Chains will expect volume discounts — have a custom quote option ready by then.
