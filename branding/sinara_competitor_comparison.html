Where Sinara is behind

  • No mobile app yet (competitors have iOS + Android)

  • No payroll integration
  • No in-app team messaging
  • No demand forecasting
  • No leave / absence management
  • Smaller user community, less social proof

Where Sinara is uniquely ahead

  • Only scheduler with built-in Japanese allergen OCR scanning
  • Auto-derives menu allergens from linked ingredients
  • True constraint optimization (OR-Tools CP-SAT) vs. simple heuristics
  • Scheduling + food safety in a single platform — no one else does this
  • Designed specifically for Japan hospitality compliance
  • Flat pricing — not per-user (huge savings for large teams)

Strategic Takeaways
★

Your moat is the allergen layer

No competitor — Deputy, 7shifts, When I Work, Sling — has any allergen or food safety feature. This is a completely uncontested space. A hotel in Japan needs this by law. You own that niche.

¥

Flat pricing is a structural advantage

Competitors charge per user. A 30-person hotel pays ¥12,000–¥21,000/month with them. With Sinara Pro at ¥4,980 flat, you're 60–75% cheaper. That's the pitch — not "we're better at scheduling," but "we cost less AND handle allergens."

↑

What to build next to close the gap

The biggest missing pieces are: (1) mobile view for staff to see their schedule, (2) shift swap / time-off requests, (3) basic labor cost tracking. These would make Sinara credible for a paid pilot.
